Who we are

A social enterprise, built on empathy

NiCE World exists for the members of our community who are too often overlooked — people doing it tough, ageing alone, or one bad month away from crisis. We trade like any business, but every dollar goes back into the people we serve.

We show up with free health checkups, plain-language insurance for medical, home and vehicle needs, and a case-by-case approach to hardship — because no two hard days look the same. When our own support isn't enough, we go looking: for the right charity, government service or community fund to fill the gap.

What we offer

Three ways we look after you

Regular, no-cost checkups delivered where people already are — community centres, outreach vans and local clinics.

Insurance you can trust

Straightforward cover, written in plain English, priced to fit a real budget.

  • Medical insurance
  • Home & contents insurance
  • Vehicle insurance

Hardship support

When someone falls into hardship, we don't just refer them on — we walk the path with them.

  • Emergency food, bills & bond assistance
  • Warm handover to partner charities
  • Ongoing check-ins until things stabilise

Hardship, handled with empathy

No one falls through the cracks on our watch

Hardship rarely arrives with a warning. A lost job, a medical bill, a relationship breakdown — any of it can tip someone into crisis. When that happens, our team listens first and works out what's actually needed, whether that's an emergency food box, help with a bill, or simply someone to call.

Where our own resources aren't enough, we lean on our network of community organisations, charities and government services to find the right support, fast.

Local community health services Emergency relief charities Financial counselling networks Government hardship programs
